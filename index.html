I am an M.S. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Rutgers University–New Brunswick, with a concentration in machine learning. I currently work with Prof. Bo Yuan on evaluating human motion in generated videos.

My research interests include evaluation of generative video models, multimodal large language models, and interpretable machine learning. I am interested in understanding what learned models do and in building evaluations whose judgments can be traced back to evidence.

I am applying to Ph.D. programs for Fall 2027.

Research

Video Generation & Evaluation

I study how to evaluate human motion in generated videos. After analyzing benchmarks such as VBench and WorldScore and testing multimodal LLMs as motion scorers, I am designing an evaluation protocol that requires visible evidence for every score.

Interpretable Models of Driving Behavior

I trained compact driver-specific recurrent networks (TinyGRUs) and distilled them into explicit, recursively executable state equations that describe individual driving behavior.
