About Research Education

Yiming Liao

I am a master’s student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Rutgers University, with a focus on machine learning. I received my B.Eng. in Communication Engineering from Beijing Jiaotong University in 2025.

My research interests include video generation evaluation and interpretable modeling of sequential behavior. My current work focuses on evaluating human motion and actions in generated videos. I have also worked on personalized driving behavior, studying how compact recurrent neural networks can be described using explicit state equations.

Research

Ongoing research

Evaluating Human Motion in Generated Videos

This project studies how to evaluate human motion and actions in generated videos. It focuses on understanding the limitations of existing evaluation methods and exploring more reliable ways to assess motion quality.

Unpublished manuscript

Can State Equations Describe Personalized Driving Behavior?

Yiming Liao, Guoyuan Wu, Xishun Liao

This project explores interpretable modeling of personalized driving behavior using TinyRNNs. It investigates how explicit state equations can describe the dynamics learned by these models and make individual driving patterns easier to understand.
