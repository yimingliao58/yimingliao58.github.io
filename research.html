Research

My work focuses on evaluating generated videos and understanding the behavior of learned models.

Video generation

Evaluating Human Motion in Generated Videos

This ongoing project studies how to evaluate human motion and actions in generated videos. It examines the limitations of existing evaluation methods and explores more reliable ways to assess motion quality.

Interpretable models

Can State Equations Describe Personalized Driving Behavior?

Yiming Liao, Guoyuan Wu, Xishun Liao

This project explores interpretable modeling of personalized driving behavior using compact recurrent neural networks (TinyRNNs). It investigates how explicit state equations can describe the dynamics learned by these models and make individual driving patterns easier to understand.
