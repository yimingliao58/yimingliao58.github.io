Video generation

Evaluating Human Motion in Generated Videos

With Prof. Bo Yuan, Rutgers University · May 2026–present

This ongoing project asks how reliably, and how transparently, automated evaluators judge human motion in generated videos.

  • Analyzed the scoring mechanisms of VBench and WorldScore to see how their video quality metrics relate to observable content and motion.
  • Evaluated multimodal LLMs as autonomous motion scorers and found score saturation and weak visual justification in the tested settings.
  • Designed a two-stage observation-and-verification protocol that ties each score on body structure, coordination, contact and support, and motion progression to traceable visual evidence. Validation against human judgments is planned.

Interpretable models

Can State Equations Describe Personalized Driving Behavior?

Yiming Liao, Guoyuan Wu, Xishun Liao

With Prof. Xishun Liao, University of Central Florida · Oct 2025–Aug 2026

This project asks whether the dynamics learned by compact recurrent models of individual drivers can be written down as explicit state equations.

  • Trained driver-specific TinyGRU models with four-dimensional hidden states on 40 trips from two drivers to predict maneuvers one second ahead.
  • Constructed explicit state-transition equations using observable-input features, ridge regression, stability-constrained state coupling, and recursive feature selection.
  • Validated the equations by full-sequence recursive rollout and five-fold trip-level cross-validation; they recover the dominant hidden-state trajectories and maneuver probabilities, while a controlled experiment exposed history-dependent effects they cannot reproduce.

Manuscript in preparation.
